# Business Analyst

**Company:** Brightline Advisory
**Team:** Client Delivery
**Line manager:** Head of Client Delivery
**Current holder:** Jonas Weber

## Role purpose
The Business Analyst gives Brightline's advice its evidence. The role analyses client data to find where processes lose time and money, builds the models behind recommendations and explains the findings in plain terms to consultants and clients. Current work includes demand forecasting for Northmere Foods and repairs backlog analysis for Ashvale Housing Trust.

## Main responsibilities
1. **Data analysis and modelling.** Collect, clean and analyse client data. Build forecasts, capacity models and cost comparisons that show where a process can improve and by how much.
2. **Supporting consultants with data.** Answer data questions from the consultants on each account, check the numbers in draft recommendations and help shape the evidence for client workshops. This includes supporting project reporting by making sure engagement figures are available and correct when the lead consultant needs them. This is a small part of the role.
3. **Client presentations of findings.** Present analysis to client teams, explain assumptions clearly and adjust models in response to their questions.
4. **Documenting models.** Record how each model works, what data it uses and how to rerun it, so that another analyst could pick it up.
5. **Internal contribution.** Take part in team meetings and share useful techniques with colleagues.

## Performance measures
1. Analysis delivered for every recommendation before it goes to the client.
2. Every model documented so another analyst can rerun it.
3. At least one client presentation of findings per month.
4. Data questions from consultants answered within two working days.
5. No material errors found by the client in any figures the Business Analyst has checked.

## What good looks like
Consultants trust the numbers and clients understand them. Recommendations stand up to challenge because the analysis behind them is sound and clearly explained. The Business Analyst spends most of the week on analysis that changes decisions, not on moving figures from one place to another.
