# Consultant

**Company:** Brightline Advisory
**Team:** Client Delivery
**Line manager:** Head of Client Delivery
**Current holder:** Priya Nair

## Role purpose
The Consultant works alongside a Senior Client Consultant on two or three client accounts, currently Ashvale Housing Trust and Kestrel Point Logistics. The role exists to do the careful groundwork behind Brightline's advice: understanding the client's current processes, preparing the materials that make workshops productive, and drafting the first version of recommendations. It is the main route into leading client work at Brightline.

## Main responsibilities
1. **Client research and analysis.** Study how the client's processes work today through interviews, site visits and the client's own documents. Summarise what was found in a form the lead consultant and the client can use.
2. **Workshop preparation and support.** Prepare process maps, exercises and agendas for client workshops. During workshops, capture what is agreed and co-lead sections as confidence grows.
3. **Drafting recommendations.** Write the first draft of recommendations after each workshop, with the evidence behind them, for the lead consultant to review.
4. **Learning and development.** Take part in coaching with the lead consultant, attend the firm's training sessions and work towards leading a client workshop independently.
5. **Internal contribution.** Take part in team meetings and help keep engagement records accurate.

## Performance measures
1. Workshop materials ready at least two working days before each session.
2. First draft of recommendations delivered within five working days of a workshop.
3. Co-lead at least one client workshop per month.
4. Positive feedback from the lead consultant on each engagement.
5. Complete the firm's facilitation training within the first year in role.

## What good looks like
Workshops run smoothly because the materials are ready and the room is well prepared. Draft recommendations need light editing, not rewriting. The Consultant takes on a larger share of each session over time and is ready to lead client work on their own.
