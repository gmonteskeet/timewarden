# Head of Client Delivery

**Company:** Brightline Advisory
**Team:** Client Delivery
**Line manager:** Managing Partner
**Current holder:** Tomas Berg

## Role purpose
The Head of Client Delivery runs the Client Delivery team and is accountable for every client engagement it delivers. The role sets priorities across the portfolio, makes sure each engagement is staffed with the right people, keeps delivery profitable and protects the quality of Brightline's advice. The Head of Client Delivery is the senior contact when a client relationship needs attention and leads the team's contribution to new business.

## Main responsibilities
1. **Team leadership and people management.** Lead the consultants and analysts in the team. Agree objectives, hold regular one to one conversations, write development plans and make staffing decisions across accounts.
2. **Client relationships and escalations.** Act as the senior Brightline contact for each client. Join steering groups at key moments, resolve concerns quickly and keep clients confident in the team.
3. **Delivery oversight and quality.** Review engagement plans, budgets and recommendations before they reach the client. Step in early when an engagement drifts from its scope, timeline or budget.
4. **New business and proposals.** Work with the partners on proposals and pricing for new and extended engagements, drawing on the team's results.
5. **Internal contribution.** Chair the weekly team review and the monthly team meeting, and represent Client Delivery in the firm's leadership discussions.

## Performance measures
1. Team utilisation of 75 percent or higher on billable client work.
2. Every client engagement delivered within 10 percent of its agreed budget.
3. Client retention of 90 percent or higher across the year.
4. Four new or extended engagements won per year.
5. Every team member has a development plan reviewed each quarter.

## What good looks like
The team spends its time where clients feel the benefit. Engagements finish on budget and lead to further work. Consultants and analysts can say clearly what they are learning and where they are heading. Problems reach the Head of Client Delivery early, while there is still time to fix them, rather than after the client has noticed.
