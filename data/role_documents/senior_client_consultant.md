# Senior Client Consultant

**Company:** Brightline Advisory
**Team:** Client Delivery
**Line manager:** Head of Client Delivery
**Current holder:** Elena Ruiz

## Role purpose
The Senior Client Consultant leads Brightline's work with a small portfolio of client accounts, currently Northmere Foods, Kestrel Point Logistics and Ashvale Housing Trust. The role exists to understand how work really flows inside each client, to turn that understanding into clear recommendations the client can act on, and to make sure the client sees lasting change. A Senior Client Consultant is the client's first point of contact at Brightline and the person they trust to tell them the truth about their operation.

## Main responsibilities
1. **Client delivery and workshops.** Design and run client workshops that uncover how processes work in practice, where they break and what the client's own people think should change. Lead the analysis that follows and turn it into practical, costed recommendations.
2. **Client relationships.** Own the day to day relationship with each client's senior team. Hold regular calls, attend steering groups, raise risks early and keep each engagement focused on the outcomes the client is paying for.
3. **Proposals and business development.** Support new and extended engagements by shaping proposals with the Head of Client Delivery, using evidence and results from live work.
4. **Coaching.** Coach the consultants and analysts on each account so that they can prepare, co-lead and eventually lead client sessions on their own. Hold a fortnightly coaching session with each of them.
5. **Internal contribution.** Take part in team meetings, share lessons across accounts and keep engagement records accurate.

## Performance measures
1. At least 70 percent of working time spent on client facing work.
2. At least two client workshops delivered per month, each rated 4 out of 5 or higher by attendees.
3. Two client proposals supported per quarter.
4. Every client recommendation accepted or actioned within 60 days of delivery.
5. Client satisfaction score of 8 out of 10 or higher on each account.

## What good looks like
Clients describe the Senior Client Consultant as the person who helped them see their own operation clearly. Junior colleagues grow in confidence on every engagement. Time goes to the client, not to the office.
