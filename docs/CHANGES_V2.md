# What changed in version 2, and why

Written Saturday 19 September 2026, 17:00 Madrid time, after the session with the make.com mentor. Read this once, then work from `AGENTS.md` and your build file.

## The change in one paragraph
Version 1 looked at one employee's week and compared what they said with what Scout saw. Version 2 is a daily routine for a whole team. Each role gets an expected split of time by topic, read from its job description and approved by the manager. Every morning each employee gets an emailed link, is interviewed about their last working day, sees a standard expected against actual summary, corrects it and submits. The manager approves. Over time Scout reviews the approved days and suggests make.com workflows, and an approved suggestion becomes a real draft scenario.

## Decisions taken in this version, with reasons
1. **Document store: Google Drive by default, not the Singular SharePoint.** Marcus has SharePoint through his employer. Connecting a hackathon make.com account to an employer's Microsoft 365 usually needs an administrator to approve the connection, and it would give a hackathon tool a route into a firm's confidential documents. Google Drive is already to hand and the scenario is identical apart from its first module. In the pitch we say "SharePoint, Google Drive or any document store". If Marcus or Gerson has a personal or trial Microsoft 365 account with nothing confidential in it, swapping is a ten minute job and the build sheet says where.
2. **Login: personal links, no passwords.** The fastest route that still gives real users and rights. The morning email link signs the person in. Rights are enforced on the server. A labelled demo sign in list lets judges switch between the manager and an employee. We avoid Supabase's own email sign in because its built in email sending is limited to a few messages an hour, which would fail in a demo.
3. **Voice: three modes behind one switch.** Text first, browser voice second, SLNG third and time boxed. The questions always come from the make.com interview scenario, so the judges see make.com doing the thinking in every mode. SLNG's hosted agent builder is not used, because it would move the thinking out of make.com.
4. **"Yesterday" is fixed to Friday 18 September 2026** and called "your last working day" on screen, because the demo happens on a Sunday.
5. **History is generated by a script, not typed by hand**, so it is consistent, repeatable and quick to change.
6. **The interview is capped at six questions** and each turn must come back within 12 seconds. A slow interview loses the room.
